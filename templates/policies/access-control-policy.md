# Access Control Policy (Template)

**Status: placeholder, full template in progress**

This template will include:

- Purpose and scope statement
- Roles and responsibilities (system owner, IT security, HR)
- Account provisioning and de-provisioning requirements, including timing standards
- Least privilege and role-based access requirements
- Multi-factor authentication requirements
- Access review cadence and documentation requirements
- Exception handling process
- Policy review and approval history table
