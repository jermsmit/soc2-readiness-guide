# About the Author

Jermal Smith is an IT leader and security champion with over ten years of experience building and running security compliance programs in the life sciences industry. A founding member of his organization's corporate security compliance function and its incident handling focal point, he has led security posture, policy, and SOC 2 compliance efforts that sustained a decade of Type II audits with zero findings. He writes and shares practical, field-tested guidance for IT and security leaders navigating SOC 2 readiness in regulated environments.
