# 1. Trust Services Criteria, Explained

**Status: outline, full content in progress**

## What this section will cover

- What the five Trust Services Criteria actually are (Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, Privacy) and how AICPA defines them
- Why almost every company only pursues Security plus one or two others, and how to decide which ones apply to you
- The difference between a "criterion" and a "control," and why conflating the two is the most common early mistake
- How the Common Criteria (CC1 through CC9) map to the Security criterion, since these form the backbone of every SOC 2 report regardless of scope
- Reading an actual SOC 2 report structure so you know what your finished product will look like before you start building toward it
- Life sciences note: why most life sciences SaaS and platform companies end up including Confidentiality given the sensitivity of clinical and research data, even when Privacy is left out of scope

## Common mistakes this section will address

- Choosing criteria based on what competitors have rather than what your product and customer contracts actually require
- Treating the criteria as a checklist instead of a description of outcomes an auditor will test
