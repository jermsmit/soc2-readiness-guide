# 6. Vendor and Third-Party Risk Management

**Status: outline, full content in progress**

## What this section will cover

- Building a vendor inventory and risk tiering model based on data access and system criticality
- What to actually request from vendors: SOC 2 reports, security questionnaires, penetration test summaries
- Reviewing a vendor's SOC 2 report for complementary user entity controls that become your responsibility
- Setting a vendor review cadence and assigning ownership so this does not silently lapse after year one
- Life sciences note: managing CRO, CDMO, and clinical data platform vendors where GxP vendor qualification and SOC 2 vendor risk review can and should share a single record

## Common mistakes this section will address

- Collecting vendor SOC 2 reports without reading them, and missing complementary controls the vendor expects you to implement
- No tiering model, so a payroll vendor and a critical infrastructure vendor get the same shallow review
