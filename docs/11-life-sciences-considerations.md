# 11. Life Sciences Specific Considerations

**Status: outline, full content in progress**

## What this section will cover

- Where SOC 2 and GxP computer system validation overlap, and where they genuinely do not
- Mapping 21 CFR Part 11 electronic records and electronic signature controls to relevant SOC 2 Common Criteria so evidence can serve both purposes
- HIPAA considerations for platforms handling protected health information alongside SOC 2 scope, and where a HIPAA-specific assessment is still required separately
- Clinical trial data handling: sponsor expectations, data integrity (ALCOA+ principles), and how they intersect with SOC 2 Processing Integrity
- Working with Quality and Regulatory Affairs teams so SOC 2 does not get built in a silo separate from existing validation programs
- Avoiding duplicate audits and duplicate evidence requests across GxP inspections, HIPAA assessments, and SOC 2 fieldwork
